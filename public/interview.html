Before you begin

Interview guidelines

How the interview runs

01Slot 1 — Interview. spoken questions about your resume and the position, asked one at a time by an AI voice, on its own clock. Answer out loud — your speech is captured automatically. There is no typing.
02Slot 2 — Reasoning. questions, fully text-based with no voice: questions.
03Each question also has its own time window. When it runs out the interview moves on by itself and you cannot return to that question. If Slot 1's clock runs out first, any remaining Slot 1 questions are skipped and Slot 2 begins.
04Finished early? Press Next question to submit and move on. In Slot 1 you can press Clear answer to re-record your answer before moving on.
05Questions are asked in order. There is no going back, and no editing an answer once submitted.
06Want to stop early? A Submit interview button is available throughout — it asks you to confirm before ending your attempt.

Proctoring rules

01Your camera and microphone stay on for the whole interview and the session is recorded for the hiring team.
02The interview runs in full screen. Leaving full screen ends it.
03Switching to another window, another tab or another application ends the interview immediately.
04Copy, paste and right-click are disabled. Do not use a second screen or a second device.
05If another person appears in your camera frame, an electronic device (phone, tablet, second screen) is seen in frame, or a second voice is heard, you get an on-screen warning and a photo is captured as evidence. After 3 such warnings for the same issue, the interview closes automatically.
06An ended interview cannot be restarted by you. Only the hiring team can issue a new link.
